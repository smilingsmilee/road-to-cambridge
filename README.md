# Road to Cambridge

An AI-powered study assistant designed for undergraduate-level proof-based mathematics.

## About

Mathematics is learned by doing, not by reading solutions.

Instead of immediately providing complete solutions, Road to Cambridge encourages students to construct rigorous proofs by offering hints, identifying gaps in reasoning, and explaining key definitions and theorems. The goal is to help students build intuition in proof-based mathematics rather than spoonfeeding them the whole proof.

*Built as a personal project to help me become a stronger mathematician and take the next step towards postgraduate study at Cambridge.*

## Stack

- **Language:** Python
- **Frontend:** Streamlit
- **LLM:** GPT (OpenAI API)
